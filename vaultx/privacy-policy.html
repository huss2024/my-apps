VaultX - Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

VaultX is designed as an offline private vault. Your saved vault entries stay on your device.

App name: VaultX

Developer name: Hussien Mohammednur Beshir

Overview

VaultX ("the App") is an offline password manager and secure notes application. This policy explains how the App handles data and privacy.

Data Stored by the App

VaultX stores the vault data that you create inside the App, such as logins, notes, card notes, usernames, passwords, and private text.

  • Your vault data is stored locally on your device.
  • Your vault data is encrypted before storage.
  • The developer does not receive, view, or store your vault contents.
  • VaultX does not provide cloud sync or online account storage.

Master Password

Your master password is used to unlock your local vault. The App does not send your master password to the developer or to a remote server. If you forget your master password, the developer cannot recover your vault.

Biometric Unlock (Optional)

If you enable fingerprint or face unlock in Settings, VaultX may store your master password in your device secure storage so you can unlock faster after device authentication. This feature is optional and stays on your device. You can turn it off at any time in Settings or by resetting the vault.

Internet and Network Use

The core vault features work offline. If advertising is enabled in a released version, Google AdMob may use network access to display ads. VaultX does not send your saved vault entries to advertising providers.

Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device and usage data for advertising, fraud prevention, and ad measurement. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

  • Expo - app framework and build tooling
  • Google AdMob - advertising, if enabled in the installed version

Children's Privacy

VaultX is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Deletion

You can delete your local vault from inside the App using the reset option in Settings. Uninstalling the App may also remove local app data depending on your device and operating system behavior.
