PDF Toolkit Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 30, 2026

PDF Toolkit is developed by Hussien Mohammednur Beshir. This privacy policy explains how PDF Toolkit handles information when you use the app.

Files and documents

PDF Toolkit lets you select PDF files and images for document tools such as opening, sharing, merging, splitting, optimizing, image-to-PDF, text-to-PDF, and selectable text extraction. These selected files are processed on your device for the requested tool. PDF Toolkit does not require an account and does not upload your documents to our own server for conversion.

Text extraction

PDF to Text is designed for PDFs that already contain selectable text. Scanned image PDFs may not contain selectable text. OCR support may be added in a future version.

Advertising

PDF Toolkit is a free app supported by limited advertising. Ads may be provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising ID and related technical information to deliver, measure, and improve ads according to Google's policies.

Data we do not collect

PDF Toolkit does not require you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, financial information, health information, messages, or calendar data.

Permissions

The app may request access to files or images only when you choose files or images for PDF tools. Camera-based OCR is not included in this version.

Children

PDF Toolkit is a general document utility and is not directed to children. The app is intended for users who can manage documents and understand advertising-supported apps.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy when the app changes. The updated policy will be posted at this page.
