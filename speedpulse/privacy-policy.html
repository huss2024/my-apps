SpeedPulse — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

Developer: Hussien Mohammednur Beshir

Overview

SpeedPulse - WiFi Speed Test ("SpeedPulse") is a free, ad-supported application that measures your internet connection speed. Your privacy is important to us.

Information We Collect

SpeedPulse does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. All speed test results are stored locally on your device only.

Internet Access

SpeedPulse requires internet access to perform speed tests. The app connects to Cloudflare's public speed test servers to measure download speed, upload speed, and latency. No personal data is sent during these tests — only generic data packets used for measurement.

Network Information

The Network Info feature uses the public ipapi.co API to display your public IP address, ISP name, and approximate location. This information is fetched on-demand and is never stored or transmitted by SpeedPulse.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad personalization and analytics. We request non-personalized ads by default. For details on data collected by Google, please see Google's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

All speed test history is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. No data is synced to the cloud. No account or registration is required. You can delete all stored data at any time from the Settings screen.

Third-Party Services

  • Cloudflare — Speed test infrastructure (Privacy Policy)
  • Google AdMob — Advertising (Privacy Policy)
  • ipapi.co — IP geolocation lookup (Privacy Policy)

Children's Privacy

SpeedPulse does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app is rated for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
