ScanBolt — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 15, 2026

Overview

ScanBolt ("the App") is a QR code, barcode, and document scanner application. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect and how we handle it.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • All scanned QR codes and barcodes are processed locally on your device.
  • Your scan history is stored only in a local database on your phone.
  • Documents you capture are saved locally to your device's storage.
  • No data is sent to our servers or any third-party service.

Camera Permission

The App requests camera access solely to scan QR codes, barcodes, and capture documents. Camera data is never recorded, uploaded, or shared.

Storage Permission

The App requests storage access to save captured documents and generated QR codes to your device's gallery. Files are stored only on your device.

Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device data for ad personalization. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements
  • Expo — for app updates and crash reporting

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
